\section{Overview}

15 Human Labelled Set papers were reviewed independently by both NCI and NHLBI. 
\textbf{9 of the 15 pairs agree on every field; the 6 below do not.}

NHLBI's labels come from two disjoint source files: papers taken to full extraction appear in
\emph{NHLBI-159} (\texttt{crt\_review\_table\_112.tex}, with a full power/data-analysis
write-up), and papers rejected before extraction appear only in \emph{NHLBI-178}
(\texttt{NHLBI\_exclusions\_178.csv}, exclusion reason only). NCI's labels come from a single
file, \emph{NCI-232} (\texttt{GroundTruthDataNCI01.xlsx}).

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Beck 2019}: the one real flip. Both institutes kept the paper, but NCI rates
  the power analysis and data analysis both correct (category A), while NHLBI-159 rates both
  incorrect. This is the only case where both institutes fully extracted the paper and reached
  opposite conclusions about its quality, the highest-priority case for a manual re-read.

  \item \textbf{Bartels 2024}: a real judgment-call disagreement. NCI excludes it as
  baseline-only (the paper reports only baseline/formative data, not trial outcomes). NHLBI-159
  disagreed with that framing, kept it, and extracted it, rating both power and data analysis
  incorrect.

  \item \textbf{King 2021}: an artifact of NCI's own dedup policy, not a disagreement about
  this paper. NCI's raw reason is literally \texttt{"random"}: NCI's team follows a policy of
  keeping only one trial per research group and randomly dropping near-duplicate papers from the
  same group. NCI's pool also contains King et al.\ 2020 (\texttt{X2MXMQA7}), which NCI kept
  instead. NHLBI never received that 2020 paper at all, so it had nothing to drop King 2021
  against, and just extracted it on its own merits.

  \item \textbf{Ockene 2021}: a related but distinct pattern. NCI's raw reason here is
  \texttt{"secondary"}, not \texttt{"random"}: NCI kept Ockene et al.\ 2018
  (\texttt{I4N8RJX7}, the trial's design/rationale paper) and treated the 2021 outcomes paper as
  a secondary analysis of that same trial, not a true duplicate. NHLBI-159 again never had the
  2018 paper, so it reviewed the 2021 paper directly.

  \item \textbf{Gilbert 2022}: both institutes exclude the paper outright; they only disagree
  on \emph{why}. NCI calls it baseline-only, NHLBI-178 calls it an implementation study. Since
  both sides land on ``excluded,'' this has no effect on the inclusion gate; it only matters if
  exclusion-reason categories themselves are being scored.

  \item \textbf{Smith 2023}: same shape as Gilbert. Both institutes exclude the paper; NCI
  calls it an implementation study, NHLBI-178 calls it a methods paper. Again, the gate outcome
  agrees; only the reason label differs.
\end{itemize}
