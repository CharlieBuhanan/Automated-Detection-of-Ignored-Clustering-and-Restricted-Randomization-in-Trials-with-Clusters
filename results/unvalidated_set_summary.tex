\section{Overview}

This document summarizes the corpus fetched from Zotero (Glykos \(\triangleright\) \emph{Boring Task}),
labeled the \textbf{Unvalidated Set} to distinguish it from the 500 human-labeled validation papers,
which arrive by a separate route and are not included here.

\section{Identifier coverage}

Zotero has no dedicated field for PMID/PMCID on older records, so these are recovered from a
combination of a dedicated field (where present), the \texttt{Extra} field, \texttt{archiveID}, and the
item URL, in that priority order. Coverage is reported below out of 1494 total papers.

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\begin{tabular}{lrr}
\toprule
Identifier & Count & Coverage \\
\midrule
DOI   & 1490 & \SI{99.7}{\percent} \\
PMID  & 1494 & \SI{100}{\percent} \\
PMCID & 1431 & \SI{95.8}{\percent} \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\caption{Identifier coverage across the Unvalidated Set. High coverage on all three identifiers
supports joining this corpus to external metadata sources by DOI or PMCID if needed.}
\end{table}

\section{Multiple PDF attachments}

172 of 1494 records (\SI{11.5}{\percent}) had more than one PDF attachment on the Zotero record. In
each case the fetch script selected the highest-priority attachment (by link mode) and logged a
warning rather than guessing silently.

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\begin{tabular}{lr}
\toprule
Category & Count \\
\midrule
Records with $>1$ PDF attachment & 172 \\
\quad Duplicate attachment (same title twice) & 167 \\
\quad Genuinely different attachments & 5 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\caption{Breakdown of multi-attachment records. The 5 records with genuinely different attachment
titles warrant manual review, since one may be a preprint or supplement rather than the published
version.}
\end{table}
