\subsection*{Why the random tie-break exclusions are removed}

The source protocol used a pseudo-random number generator to pick one manuscript whenever several
shared a first author, in order to reduce the influence of very productive groups. That decision
depends on which \emph{other} papers happen to be in the collection, so no model reading a single PDF
can reproduce it. Scoring the model against those 31 papers would impose a hard ceiling near 94\% on
exclusion accuracy and penalise it for a coin flip. Same-first-author de-duplication, if wanted, is
applied afterwards as a deterministic pass over the model's survivors, with a recorded seed.

\subsection*{The two institutes did not use the same exclusion criteria}

NHLBI excluded 72.9\% of what it reviewed (218/299); NCI excluded 56.7\% (122/215). Most of the
16-point gap is accounted for by seven exclusion categories that appear in the NHLBI labels but not in
the published NCI protocol.

Removing those 41 papers brings the NHLBI exclusion rate to 59.2\%, against NCI's 56.7\%. That near
convergence is strong evidence the gap is a protocol difference rather than rater disagreement. It has
not been confirmed with the study team, and the exclusion rubric cannot be finalised until it is:
\emph{stepped wedge design} in particular is a cluster randomized design, so excluding nine such trials
reads more like a labelling error than a criterion.

\section{The Patterson citations}

Two label rows cite \texttt{(Patterson et al., 2022a)} and \texttt{(2022b)}. APA appends the
\texttt{a}/\texttt{b} suffix only when two references share an author and year, so the suffix records
that the labeller could not separate them either. Both citations resolve to the same underlying study:
\texttt{IT2B87LL}, the article, and \texttt{JBUFJCLU}, a \emph{Correction to:} that article which has
already been dropped. Only one real paper is affected. Both label rows carry identical labels
(excluded, random tie-break), so this is a counting problem rather than a correctness one. The fix is
to teach the citation join to skip candidates already marked dropped; it is held pending the policy
decision on whether correction notices belong in the corpus at all.

\section{Papers now in neither set}

would be silently absent from the study.
